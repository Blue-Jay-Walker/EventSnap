Privacy Policy for EventSnap

This application is designed with privacy and security in mind. Please review how your data is handled below:

No Data Storage

The App does not store any data. The events are stored directly in YOUR Google calendar. Login is handled securely by Google Login (OAuth).

Public LLM Processing

The event URLs or Text you submit will be interpreted by a public LLM, in this case OpenAI. Do not submit highly sensitive personal text.

Dedicated Calendar

A new calendar named "Events to Decide" will be created and used to add events. Your main Calendar remains completely untouched. You can even use a different Google account if you want.

Full Control

You can revoke the permissions from your Google account anytime for EventSnap via your Google Account settings.
